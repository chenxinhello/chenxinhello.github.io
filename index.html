🥳 逻辑 | Logic Logica Logika & ...

Let no one ignorant of logic enter.
(paraphrase of the sign on the gate to the Academy at Athens)

I love logic, especially Modal Logic and its “peripherals", and “I Want to Be a Logician" (paraphrase of the title I Want to Be a Mathematician by Paul Halmos).

Don't just read it; fight it! Ask your own questions, look for your own examples, discover your own proofs. Is the hypothesis necessary? Is the converse true? What happens in the classical special case? What about the degenerate cases? Where does the proof use the hypothesis? (P. Halmos, I Want to Be a Mathematician)
The fear of stumbling makes the mind hold tightly onto the handrail of logic. (André Gide, Les Nouvelles Nourritures)
If people do not believe that mathematics is simple, it is only because they do not realize how complicated life is. (von Neumann)
If you zip through a page in less than an hour, you are probably going too fast. (Sheldon Axler: Linear Algebra Done Right)
